## New
- First public **v1.0.0-rc1** prerelease of the native PS Vita port of *Dragon Quest IV: Chapters of the Chosen* (Android v1.0.5). The Android game libraries run unchanged on the Vita, without Unity. No game data is included.
- Landscape display (`layout=portrait` restores the original), physical buttons plus touch, and English, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese text.

## Status
- Development and testing so far were done in the Vita3K emulator, where towns, shops, the church, the inn, the world map and battles were played. The prologue and most of the story have not been played through.
- On a real Vita, only the start-up has been checked: this build boots through the intro credits to the title menu at 30 FPS. Nothing has been played on hardware yet, and sound has never been checked by ear.
- Korean and Chinese text, and performance and memory during play on a real console, are unverified. Expect bugs, and keep backups of your saves.

## Installation
- Install **dqivn.vpk** and [kubridge](https://github.com/bythos14/kubridge/releases/). [FdFix](https://github.com/TheOfficialFloW/FdFix/releases/) is recommended unless you use rePatch.
- Convert your own **Google Play v1.0.5 APK and OBB `main.106`** with the [web converter](https://frangarcj.github.io/dqiv_vita/) or `tools/make_data.py`, then copy the result to `ux0:data/dqiv/`. See the [README](https://github.com/frangarcj/dqiv_vita#setup-end-users) for the full steps.
