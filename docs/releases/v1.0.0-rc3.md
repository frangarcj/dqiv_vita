## Fixed
- **Yes/No choices hidden below the screen.** Some questions, such as the inn's "Will you be staying?", put their Yes / No buttons below the bottom edge of the screen, so only two thin lines were visible. The dialogue box now moves up to make room for the buttons.
- **Touching hidden spell buttons.** In the Magic menu, after looking at a character with spells, a character without spells (Ragnar) still reacted to touches on the empty spell list. You got a "Cast" screen and then an error. Hidden buttons no longer react to touch. Playing with the physical buttons was not affected.

## Status
- Both fixes come from a tester's report on a real Vita (about 4 hours of play without a crash). They were checked in the Vita3K emulator, **not yet on a real Vita**. All 31 scripted test scenarios (towns, shops, inn, church, battles, world map) ran on an optimised build again.
- Sound has never been checked by ear. Most of the story has not been played through by the developer.
- Korean and Chinese text, and performance and memory during long play on a real console, are unverified. Expect bugs, and keep backups of your saves.

## Installation
- Updating from rc1 or rc2: install the new **dqivn.vpk**. Your converted data and saves in `ux0:data/dqiv/` stay as they are.
- New install: install **dqivn.vpk**, [kubridge](https://github.com/bythos14/kubridge/releases/) and [FdFix](https://github.com/TheOfficialFloW/FdFix/releases/) (skip FdFix if you already use rePatch). Convert your own **Google Play v1.0.5 APK and OBB `main.106`** with the [web converter](https://frangarcj.github.io/dqiv_vita/) or `tools/make_data.py`, then copy the result to `ux0:data/dqiv/`. See the [README](https://github.com/frangarcj/dqiv_vita#installation) for the full steps.
